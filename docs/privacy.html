Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 16, 2026

Welcome to Living Simulator AI ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe and transparent gaming experience. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Living Simulator AI is designed to minimize personal data collection:

  • Local Gameplay Data: Your simulator choices, character stats, wealth balance, life events, custom avatars, and progress are stored locally on your device using Apple's secure local device storage. We do not transmit your personal gameplay decisions to external private tracking servers.
  • Device and Usage Information: When you access the app, basic non-personally identifiable diagnostic information (such as iOS version, device model, and crash reports) may be collected automatically to improve performance and stability.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

To provide simulation features, monetization, and in-app advertising, we integrate reputable third-party SDKs that may collect data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to deliver banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. Google may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers (such as IDFA if permitted by App Tracking Transparency, or identifier for vendor), IP address, and interaction data to serve ads. You can review Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Apple StoreKit & In-App Purchases: All subscription processing (Weekly, Monthly, Yearly plans) is executed directly by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive, store, or have access to your credit card, bank account, or Apple ID payment details.

3. In-App Subscriptions (Living Simulator AI PRO)

Living Simulator AI offers optional auto-renewing subscriptions (weekly_life at $4.99/week, monthly_life at $14.99/month, and yearly_life at $99.99/year). Subscription verification and receipts are processed securely by Apple StoreKit.

4. Data Retention and Security

We implement standard security measures to protect your gameplay information. Since game progression is retained locally on your device, you can delete your data at any time by resetting your game progress from the in-app settings menu or by uninstalling the application.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Living Simulator AI is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we will promptly take steps to delete that data.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable legal requirements. The updated date at the top of this page indicates when the latest modifications became effective.
